Smart Farming Tips

Expert farming techniques to increase yield, reduce costs, and build a sustainable farm

🌿
ORGANIC

Start Organic Farming

Transition to organic farming gradually — start with 20% of land. Use vermicompost, neem cake, bio-fertilizers. Get PGS-India certification for 20-30% premium prices.

💰 Benefit: Up to 30% higher income
♻️
ORGANIC

Vermicompost Production

Set up a vermicompost unit with 50 kg earthworms. Feed farm waste (crop residue, cow dung). Produces 2-3 tonnes of compost/month. Saves ₹15,000+ on fertilizers annually.

💰 Benefit: Save ₹15,000/year on fertilizers
🪲
PEST CONTROL

Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

Combine biological (Trichogramma cards), chemical (minimal), and cultural methods. Install pheromone traps at 5 per acre. Reduces pesticide use by 40-60% with better results.

💰 Benefit: 40% reduction in pesticide costs
🌿
PEST CONTROL

Neem-Based Pest Control

Prepare neem spray: 5kg neem leaves in 10L water, boil, strain, dilute 1:20. Apply weekly as preventive treatment. Effective against 200+ pest species naturally.

💰 Benefit: Zero cost natural pesticide
💧
IRRIGATION

Drip Irrigation Setup

Install drip irrigation with 90% government subsidy (PMKSY). Saves 50% water compared to flood irrigation. Increases yield by 20-30%. Reduces weed growth significantly.

💰 Benefit: 50% water savings + higher yield
🌧️
IRRIGATION

Farm Pond Construction

Build a farm pond (60x40x3m) for rainwater harvesting. 50% subsidy available under MNREGA. Stores 7,200 cubic meters — enough for 5 acres for one season.

💰 Benefit: Water security for dry months
🌍
SOIL HEALTH

Crop Rotation for Soil Health

Follow cereals with legumes: Rice→Pulses→Wheat→Vegetables. Legumes fix 50-100 kg Nitrogen/hectare naturally. Reduces fertilizer requirement by 25% over 3 years.

💰 Benefit: 25% less fertilizer needed
♻️
SOIL HEALTH

Mulching for Soil Moisture

Apply 5-6 cm mulch (straw, dry leaves, plastic) around crop rows. Reduces water evaporation by 40%, controls weeds, moderates soil temperature, and adds organic matter.

💰 Benefit: 40% less irrigation needed
📱
TECHNOLOGY

Digital Farming Apps

Use KrishiSathi AI for crop predictions. Download government apps: Kisan Suvidha, mKisan, Crop Insurance App (AIDE). Register on eNAM for direct market access.

💰 Benefit: Better prices + government services
🤖
TECHNOLOGY

Drone Usage in Farming

Use drones for crop monitoring, pesticide spraying, and yield estimation. Agricultural drones now available on rent at ₹500-800/acre. Sprays 10 acres/hour vs 2 hours manual.

💰 Benefit: 5x faster spraying, less chemical use
🌾
ORGANIC

Intercropping for More Income

Grow two crops simultaneously: Cotton + Urad Dal, Sugarcane + Onion, Maize + Cowpea. Intercropping increases land productivity by 20-40% with minimal extra input.

💰 Benefit: 20-40% more income from same land
🦜
PEST CONTROL

Bird Perch for Natural Pest Control

Install T-shaped bamboo perches (10-15 per acre) at 1m height in the field. Birds eat stem borers, caterpillars, grasshoppers. Reduces pest damage by 15-20% naturally.

💰 Benefit: Free biological pest control

⚡ Daily Farming Reminders

🌅 Morning: Inspect crops for pest signs. Water in early morning (5-8 AM) for low evaporation.
🌤️ Afternoon: Avoid pesticide spraying in peak heat. Record weather & crop observations.
🌙 Evening: Second watering if needed. Check pheromone traps. Plan next day's activities.
📅 Weekly: Foliar nutrition spray, field bund inspection, market price check.
