Alluvial Soil

जलोढ़ मिट्टी | Most Fertile

Found in the Indo-Gangetic plains — the most fertile and agriculturally important soil in India. Covers 43% of total land area.

📍 Found In:

Punjab, Haryana, UP, Bihar, West Bengal, Assam, River deltas of AP & Odisha

⚗️ Characteristics:
• Rich in potash, phosphoric acid
• pH: 7.0–8.5 (slightly alkaline)
• High water retention capacity
• Sandy loam to clay texture
• Low in nitrogen & organic matter
🌾 Rice 🌽 Wheat 🎋 Sugarcane 🌿 Cotton 🥜 Oilseeds 🌽 Maize
🧪 Fertilizer: Add organic manure & nitrogen. Apply DAP for phosphorus. Zinc sulfate for micro-nutrient deficiency.
⚫

Black Soil (Regur)

काली मिट्टी | Cotton Belt

Also known as Regur or Cotton Soil. Self-ploughing nature — cracks in dry weather absorbing organic matter. Covers Deccan Plateau.

📍 Found In:

Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Chhattisgarh

⚗️ Characteristics:
• Rich in lime, iron & magnesium
• pH: 6.5–8.0 (slightly acidic to neutral)
• High moisture retention (self-mulching)
• Cracks deeply in summer heat
• Low in phosphorus & nitrogen
🌿 Cotton 🫘 Soybean 🌻 Sunflower 🌽 Sorghum 🎋 Sugarcane 🌿 Tobacco
🧪 Fertilizer: Apply phosphorus & nitrogen. Avoid excess irrigation. Use gypsum for soil structure improvement.
🔴

Red Soil

लाल मिट्टी | Peninsular India

Red color due to iron oxide content. Found in the peninsular regions of India on crystalline and metamorphic rocks.

📍 Found In:

Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, parts of Maharashtra & MP

⚗️ Characteristics:
• Rich in iron, poor in nitrogen
• pH: 6.0–7.5 (slightly acidic)
• Low water retention
• Porous, friable texture
• Deficient in lime, phosphorus & humus
🥜 Groundnut 🌾 Millets 🫘 Pulses 🥔 Potato 🌽 Maize 🌿 Tobacco
🧪 Fertilizer: Apply FYM/compost, lime to reduce acidity. Add phosphorus & nitrogen. Drip irrigation recommended.
🟠

Laterite Soil

लेटराइट मिट्टी | Tropical Forests

Formed by intense leaching under tropical rainy conditions. High in iron & aluminum. Poor natural fertility but supports tropical plantation crops.

📍 Found In:

Kerala, Karnataka (Western Ghats), Odisha, Assam, Meghalaya, parts of Andhra Pradesh & Tamil Nadu

⚗️ Characteristics:
• Rich in iron & aluminum oxides
• pH: 5.0–6.5 (acidic)
• Very low in N, P, K, Ca, Mg
• Coarse texture, poor water holding
• Not suitable for cereal crops
☕ Tea ☕ Coffee 🌴 Rubber 🌿 Cashew 🥥 Coconut 🍍 Pineapple
🧪 Fertilizer: Heavy organic matter addition essential. Apply lime to neutralize acidity. Green manuring with Dhaincha helps.
🟡

Sandy / Desert Soil

रेतीली मिट्टी | Arid Zones

Found in arid and semi-arid regions. Very low organic content but good drainage. Requires irrigation and organic matter for cultivation.

📍 Found In:

Rajasthan (Thar Desert), Gujarat (Rann of Kutch), parts of Haryana, Punjab & UP

⚗️ Characteristics:
• Low organic matter & nutrients
• pH: 7.5–9.0 (alkaline)
• Very low water retention
• High wind erosion risk
• Rich in soluble salts & phosphate
🌾 Millets (Bajra) 🥜 Groundnut 🫘 Pulses 🌵 Cluster bean 🌰 Moth bean
🧪 Fertilizer: Heavy FYM application. Drip irrigation essential. Windbreaks/shelterbelts protect soil. Use gypsum for sodic soils.
🔵

Mountain / Forest Soil

पर्वतीय मिट्टी | Himalayan Belt

Found in hilly and mountainous regions. Rich in organic matter from forest litter. Ideal for temperate fruits and spices.

📍 Found In:

Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, J&K, Northeast India, Nilgiris (Tamil Nadu), Western Ghats

⚗️ Characteristics:
• High organic matter (humus-rich)
• pH: 5.0–7.0 (acidic to neutral)
• Good moisture holding
• Rich in N, Ca but low in P & K
• Variable texture with altitude
🍎 Apple 🍑 Peach ☕ Tea 🌿 Spices 🌾 Wheat 🥔 Potato
🧪 Fertilizer: Apply potash & phosphorus. Contour farming prevents erosion. Organic farming works best here.
