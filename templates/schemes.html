🏛️ Government Welfare

Farmer Schemes & Benefits

All important central and state government schemes for Indian farmers — explained clearly with application links

🌾

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

Provides ₹6,000/year income support to all small and marginal farmers directly in 3 installments of ₹2,000 each.

  • ₹2,000 transferred every 4 months directly to bank account
  • No middle-man — direct benefit transfer (DBT)
  • Covers all land-holding farmers across India
  • 17th installment recently released (2024-25)
  • Register online or at Common Service Centre (CSC)
📋 Documents needed: Aadhaar, land records (Khasra/Khatauni), bank passbook, mobile number
Apply / Check Status →
🛡️

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Comprehensive crop insurance scheme providing financial support if crops fail due to natural calamities, pests or diseases.

  • Kharif crops: Only 2% premium from farmer
  • Rabi crops: Only 1.5% premium from farmer
  • Horticulture/commercial crops: Max 5% premium
  • Full sum insured coverage — govt pays remaining premium
  • Claim settlement within 2 months of harvest
📋 Apply at: Nearest bank, insurance company, CSC, or online at pmfby.gov.in before crop season cutoff date
Apply for Insurance →
🧪

Soil Health Card Scheme

Free soil testing for all farmers to know their soil's nutrient status and get customized fertilizer recommendations.

  • Free soil testing at government labs
  • Report includes 12 soil health parameters
  • Customized fertilizer & crop recommendations
  • Issued every 2 years per farmer
  • Helps reduce fertilizer costs by 20-30%
📋 Apply at: Nearest Krishi Kendra, block agriculture office, or online
Get Soil Card →
💳

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Flexible short-term credit facility for farmers to meet agricultural and allied activity expenses at subsidized interest rates.

  • Credit up to ₹3 lakh at only 7% interest/year
  • Additional 3% subvention for timely repayment (4% net)
  • Flexible repayment aligned with crop harvest cycle
  • Covers crop cultivation, post-harvest, household needs
  • Available from all major banks (SBI, cooperative banks)
📋 Documents: Aadhaar, land records, bank account, passport photo, crop details
Apply for KCC →
💧

PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY)

"More Crop Per Drop" — promotes efficient irrigation to conserve water and improve agricultural productivity.

  • 90% subsidy on drip irrigation for small/marginal farmers
  • 80% subsidy on sprinkler irrigation systems
  • Watershed development and water harvesting
  • Fund for micro-irrigation expansion
  • Reduces water use by 40-50%
📋 Apply at: District agriculture office or state horticulture department
Apply for Subsidy →
🏪

e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)

Online trading platform for agricultural commodities connecting farmers directly with buyers for better prices.

  • Sell produce online at competitive prices
  • Connected to 1,361+ mandis across India
  • Transparent price discovery mechanism
  • Reduce middlemen and improve farmer income
  • Payment directly to farmer bank account
📋 Register at: enam.gov.in | Helpline: 1800-270-0224 (Toll Free)
Register on eNAM →
🍎

Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH)

Holistic development of horticulture sector including fruits, vegetables, mushroom, spices, flowers and plantation crops.

  • 50% subsidy on planting material & nursery
  • Subsidies on protected cultivation (poly-houses)
  • Support for cold storage and pack houses
  • Assistance for organic certification
  • Technology intervention for yield improvement
📋 Apply at: State Horticulture Mission office or district horticulture officer
Apply for MIDH →
🐄

Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Fund

Support for setting up dairy processing, meat processing, animal feed plants and breed improvement facilities.

  • 3% interest subvention on loans
  • Coverage for dairy, poultry, piggery enterprises
  • NABARD support for rural livestock activities
  • Namdhari breed improvement support
  • Credit guarantee facility available
📋 Apply through: Scheduled commercial banks or cooperative banks with DPIIT registration
